ShieldHer Safety System

Women and child safety has become a major concern in today's society. ShieldHer is designed to provide instant protection using modern web technology — combining real-time SOS alerts, live GPS location sharing, and automatic emergency notifications.

With just one tap, users can send their location to trusted contacts via SMS, WhatsApp, and Email simultaneously — powered by Twilio. The shake-to-SOS feature allows hands-free emergency activation when a user cannot manually interact with the screen.

This system also features an AI-powered chatbot (ShieldBot) for instant safety guidance, a personal emergency contact list, safety tips, and alert history tracking — all in one seamless app.

The goal: empower every woman to feel secure, confident, and always connected to help.

✅ Features

  • One-tap SOS with SMS + WhatsApp + Email (Twilio)
  • Live GPS location tracking (Leaflet maps)
  • Shake-to-SOS hands-free detection
  • Emergency siren
  • Personal emergency contacts
  • Alert history log
  • ShieldBot AI chatbot
  • Safety tips & national helpline quick dial
  • Profile with photo upload
  • Secure user authentication
